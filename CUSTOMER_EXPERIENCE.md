# Customer experience: rental MVP

## Refined brief

Build a car-rental experience where anyone can browse cars, prices and details without an account. Require an account to submit a booking or save a favourite. After signup, sign the customer in automatically and open a personal My rentals dashboard. Let customers browse cars, choose rental dates, follow their own bookings, revisit saved cars, and maintain their profile. Keep fleet operations, customer management and payment administration in a separate staff area.

## Why keep accounts?

For this MVP, an account gives each booking and saved car a clear owner, and gives returning customers a private place to find their rental history. Browsing remains public so visitors can see the cars and prices before deciding to register. Guest checkout could be a later alternative, but it would need a separate way for guests to securely retrieve and manage their reservations.

## Main journeys

- **New customer:** browse → select a car → create an account → My rentals → continue the selected booking or explore cars.
- **Returning customer:** sign in → My rentals; if sign-in started from a booking, continue that booking.
- **Save for later:** sign in → Save car → see it in Saved cars and on My rentals → open the car and choose dates when ready.
- **Follow a rental:** My rentals → current/next booking or booking history → dates, status, payments and return details.

The dashboard shows an inviting browse action for a new customer, a current rental or next booking when there is one, and a shortlist of saved cars. Saving does not reserve a car; availability is checked when the customer chooses dates and books. Unlisted favourites remain removable, with a clear unavailable message.

## Scope boundaries

This is car rental, not a ride-hailing service: customers reserve a vehicle for a date range. Staff review bookings, prepare vehicles, record manual payments and process returns. Customers see only their own records and cannot manage fleet records, other customers or payment administration. There is no automatic card charge, guest checkout, live driver tracking or social feed in this MVP.
